Mollie rates are standard UK published rates sourced from mollie.com/gb/pricing: UK consumer cards (Visa/MC debit and credit) 1.20% + £0.20, EU/commercial/international 2.90% + £0.20, Amex 2.50% + £0.20, Pay by Bank 0.90% + £0.20. Shopify Tax fee modelled at monthly equivalent of the £5,000/yr annual cap. Volume pricing from Mollie available above £50k/month — contact Mollie sales for a quote. Alcohol merchants must provide a UK personal licence and premises licence at onboarding. Always verify rates before making commercial decisions.
